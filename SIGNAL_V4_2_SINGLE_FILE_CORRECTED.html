08 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is SIGNAL a social media agency?

Not in the traditional sense. Content is an output. SIGNAL combines research, verification, analysis, narrative development and visual communication.

Do you only cover politics?

No. Our focus includes geopolitics, geo-economics, AI, technology, cybersecurity, defense, policy and strategic risk.

Do clients need to provide research?

Not necessarily. SIGNAL can work from a brief and conduct open-source research appropriate to the assignment.

Do you conduct secret or unauthorized intelligence operations?

No. Research is based on lawful, open and verifiable information. We do not access private systems, accounts or restricted information without authorization.

Can services be customized?

Yes. Scope, depth, frequency and deliverables can be tailored to the client's objectives.
